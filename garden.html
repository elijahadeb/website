the garden

a second brain, kept in public: distilled notes from papers, manuals, and the machine itself — mapped by concept, corrected in place, never apologised for. the log records what happened; the garden holds what i currently believe is true.

the planter. a stake for every note, standing in the order that keeps linked notes nearest each other. the pale threads under the soil are the links: the further two notes stand apart, the deeper their thread runs.

six seeds and nothing planted. a seed is a thing i keep circling — read about, half-understand, and cannot yet write down without hedging. it becomes a note the day the engine forces the lesson and i can defend it in three sentences.
